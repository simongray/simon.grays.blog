# Now: August, 2026

I am currently working on this blog (**again**), mainly iterating on its intentionally skeuomorphic design + ironing out of few issues with the [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org/IndieWeb) integration which I expanded recently. The blog did have a *few* of the most basic IndieWeb features, but for a couple of years I couldn't find the time and motivation to do more. Maybe that's just called being a dad?

I did use some leftover Claude tokens in my summer holiday to get the ball rolling again; this seriously helped *a lot*.

Now it's good enough... even brings in Mastodon comments thanks to [Bridgy Fed](https://indieweb.org/Bridgy_Fed) which is one the best new features IMO.

I also think web sign-in works *in theory*, but I can't test it properly as I still need to work on **my own** [web sign-in](https://indieweb.org/Web_sign-in). This honestly seems way too complex to implement for a concept that appears fairly straightforward.

Oh, and I got back into **actually writing blog posts**! I somehow forgot to do that too after getting this domain name back in 2023. Well, I technically didn't forget (it was always on my mind), but I had trouble publishing stuff since I didn't feel like the blog was finished yet.

I'm planning on participating in the [IndieWeb carnival](https://indieweb.org/IndieWeb_Carnival) to inspire me to post some more.
